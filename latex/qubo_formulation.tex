\section*{QUBO Formulation}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────

The goal is to select a subset of AP--MP pairs that maximizes spatial
informativeness while minimizing redundancy, subject to device budgets
on the number of deployed APs and MPs.

\medskip

The objective is formulated as a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary
Optimization (QUBO) problem:

\begin{equation}
    Q(\mathbf{x},\, \alpha)
    \;=\;
    -\,\alpha \sum_{p} I_p\, x_p
    \;+\;
    (1 - \alpha) \sum_{p \neq q} R_{pq}\, x_p\, x_q
    \label{eq:qubo}
\end{equation}

\noindent
The first term rewards selecting \emph{informative} pairs (high variance
of RSS across fingerprint locations). The second term penalizes selecting
\emph{redundant} pairs (highly correlated RSS vectors). The parameter
$\alpha$ controls the trade-off between the two objectives and is
determined via binary search.

\medskip

The optimal solution is:
\begin{equation}
    \mathbf{x}^* \;=\; \arg\min_{\mathbf{x} \in \{0,1\}^{P}}\;
    Q(\mathbf{x},\, \alpha)
\end{equation}

\section*{Table of Notations}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────

\begin{table}[h!]
\centering
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.5}
\begin{tabular}{>{\bfseries}l p{10cm}}
\toprule
Symbol & Description \\
\midrule
$N$
    & Total number of candidate sensor node locations
      (shared pool for APs and MPs). \\
$K$
    & Number of fingerprint locations (distinct user positions
      in the training database). \\
$P = N(N-1)$
    & Total number of valid AP--MP pairs (all off-diagonal pairs). \\
$p,\, q$
    & Variable indices in $\{0, \ldots, P-1\}$, each mapping to a
      unique off-diagonal pair $(i,j)$ with $i \neq j$. \\
$\mathbf{x} \in \{0,1\}^{P}$
    & Binary decision vector. $x_p = 1$ means the pair $(i,j)$
      indexed by $p$ is selected; $x_p = 0$ otherwise. \\
$\text{RSS}(k,\, i,\, j)$
    & Received Signal Strength (dBm) measured at fingerprint
      location $k$, with AP at node $i$ and MP at node $j$. \\
$I_p$
    & Importance of pair $p = (i,j)$: variance of RSS over all
      fingerprint locations,
      $I_p = \mathrm{Var}_{k}\bigl[\,\text{RSS}(k,i,j)\,\bigr]$. \\
$R_{pq}$
    & Redundancy between pairs $p$ and $q$: absolute Pearson
      correlation between their RSS vectors across $K$ locations,
      $R_{pq} = \bigl|\,\mathrm{corr}\bigl(\text{RSS}(\cdot,i,j),\,
      \text{RSS}(\cdot,i',j')\bigr)\,\bigr| \in [0,\,1]$. \\
$\alpha \in [0,\,1]$
    & Trade-off parameter. Higher $\alpha$ favours importance
      (more pairs selected); lower $\alpha$ penalises redundancy
      (fewer, more complementary pairs). Found via binary search. \\
$n$
    & Maximum number of AP nodes that can be deployed (AP budget). \\
$m$
    & Maximum number of MP nodes that can be deployed (MP budget). \\
$\alpha^*$
    & Optimal trade-off value: the largest feasible $\alpha$ found
      by binary search such that AP budget and MP budget are both
      satisfied. \\
$\mathbf{x}^*$
    & Optimal binary solution corresponding to $\alpha^*$: the
      feasible solution with the lowest KNN localization error
      observed during binary search. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Summary of notation used in the QUBO formulation.}
\end{table}
